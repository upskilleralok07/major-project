The problem

Reports are scattered.
Finding one is stressful.

Patients collect reports from different hospitals, laboratories and doctors. They end up in email attachments, WhatsApp chats, paper folders and phone galleries. When an older report is needed, finding it is difficult and time-consuming.

{{ icon('folder') }}Reports stored in many different places
{{ icon('search-x') }}Old reports are hard to find quickly
{{ icon('clock') }}No clear history of visits and tests
{{ icon('share') }}Sharing with doctors is insecure and permanent
The solution

One centralised, searchable medical vault

MediVault keeps every report in one organised place, builds your medical history automatically, and lets you share individual reports with doctors for a limited time only.

Security

Built with privacy in mind

  • {{ icon('check') }} Passwords are hashed with Werkzeug – never stored in plain text
  • {{ icon('check') }} Every report request is authorised on the server
  • {{ icon('check') }} Doctors see only reports explicitly shared with them
  • {{ icon('check') }} Expired or revoked shares are blocked by the backend
  • {{ icon('check') }} File type, MIME type, content and size are validated
  • {{ icon('check') }} CSRF protection on every form
